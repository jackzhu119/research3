## 2. Exact national exemption provision

The source is the jointly issued **《涉及人的生命科学和医学研究伦理审查办法》**, document **国卫科教发〔2023〕4号**, dated18 February 2023, by the National Health Commission, Ministry of Education, Ministry of Science and Technology and National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine. The [Chinese central government's official full text](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2023-02/28/content_5743658.htm) was downloaded successfully today; the [Fujian provincial science department's official republication](https://kjt.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/kycx/zcfg_39564/202310/t20231011_6271539.htm) independently reproduces the same text. The [NHC original source](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/qjjys/c100016/202302/6b6e447b3edc4338856c9a652a85f44b.shtml) remains indexed but returned412 to direct requests; that access limit is recorded, not treated as evidence the document is unavailable or invalid.

Article 32's governing conditions are:

> 使用人的信息数据或者生物样本开展以下情形的涉及人的生命科学和医学研究，不对人体造成伤害、不涉及敏感个人信息或者商业利益的，可以免除伦理审查，以减少科研人员不必要的负担，促进涉及人的生命科学和医学研究开展。

The relevant listed category, **Article 32(1)**, is:

> （一）利用合法获得的公开数据，或者通过观察且不干扰公共行为产生的数据进行研究的；

Thus lawfully obtained public-data research is an enumerated category, **subject to the governing conditions**. Public availability alone is not an automatic blanket exemption. The word“可以” permits exemption under those conditions; it does not declare that every public EEG dataset, every use or every identifiable health record is automatically exempt.

Article 32(2), concerning anonymous information, is a separate category. It need not be invoked here, and the manuscript should not convert participant codes or removal of names into a claim of legally irreversible anonymization. Article 31's simplified review is also distinct from Article 32 exemption. Article 54 states that the Measures take effect upon publication; no sunset date is specified in the retrieved text.

The document is a national departmental measure/normative policy, not a statute enacted by the National People's Congress. In English use the document title and Article 32(1); do not call it a newly enacted Chinese“law” or invent a2026revision.

## 4. Manuscript wording after the author's applicability confirmation

The author has now confirmed that the study uses the national exemption basis. Record that as the **author's applicability attestation**, while citing the provision. It is distinct from a claim that CQMU's ethics committee issued a project-specific waiver.

Recommended final English text under the journal's research-ethics declaration heading:

> **Research ethics:** This study was a secondary computational analysis of lawfully obtained public EEG datasets and involved no new participant recruitment, contact or intervention. Ethical review was not required under Article 32(1) of the *Measures for Ethical Review of Life Science and Medical Research Involving Human Subjects* (Guo Wei Ke Jiao Fa [2023] No. 4), as the conditions for public-data secondary research specified in that Article were met. The ethics and consent procedures for the original EEG recordings are documented in the cited dataset publications.

If a fully explicit statement of the Article's conditions is useful, replace the middle clause with:

> The secondary analysis met the Article's conditions concerning bodily harm, sensitive personal information and commercial interests.

Keep the original studies' documented consent/approval facts and citations. Do not claim that the current authors recruited participants or obtained the original consents. The user's no-conflict and no-external-funding attestations can be stated in their proper declarations; no funding alone is not used as a logical substitute for the Article's commercial-interest condition.

Do **not** write“approved by the Ethics Committee of Chongqing Medical University”,“the Committee granted a waiver”, a project number, approval date, or institution-specific determination that has not been provided. The national exemption concerns **ethical review**, not exemption from ethical duties or a fabricated institutional“ethics determination”. Article 32 itself does not impose a universally specified exemption-letter format, so lack of such a letter is not converted into an additional prerequisite in this writing task. No further permission question or documentary demand is needed to use the author-confirmed national exemption statement accurately.
