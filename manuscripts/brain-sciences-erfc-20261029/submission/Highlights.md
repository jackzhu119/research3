# Highlights

## What are the main findings?

- ERFC uses a support-gain-retaining function set and query-specific completion bounds to decide whether to modify an EEG prediction.
- Paired exploratory analyses show fewer newly introduced errors and fewer repaired anchor errors than a matched point-fit correction.

## What are the implications of the main findings?

- Calibration can be evaluated as a selective intervention, with its correction and preservation effects reported separately.
- Scarce-label adaptation benefits from an explicit definition of when retaining an established prediction is the prescribed decision.
