4. Support-only cross-fitting, shrinkage and robust stacking.
5. Geometric coupling of rotations and gains.
6. Curvature-shape prediction averaging and an external Pan evaluation.
7. Finite-window spatiotemporal Lie adaptation, classifier capacity and source-relative risk.
8. Partial covariance transport and covariance-preserving class-direction rotation.
9. Source-residual Gaussian-process adaptation and support-information anchoring.
10. Evidence-preserving query-function calibration.
11. Prefix-to-future score transport.
12. Controlled mechanism experiments.
13. A subsequent held-out validation study whose precise independence and task definition must be taken from its own plan and evaluation receipts.

A later iteration cannot be labelled an independent replication merely because its code or directory name changed.
