## AI-assisted writing: the exact boundary

The current [MDPI Research and Publication Ethics policy](https://www.mdpi.com/ethics) distinguishes superficial editing from substantive generation. Grammar, spelling, punctuation, formatting and text-editing support can be exempt. Generating draft prose, study design, analysis, interpretation, data or graphics triggers disclosure during submission; the Methods describes use and the Acknowledgments identifies the tool/product details. The authors remain responsible, and AI tools cannot be authors.

This task requests literature synthesis, development of the argument, original prose and interpretation of research results. It therefore extends beyond the editing exemption. Natural, precise prose is compatible with this rule; removing a required disclosure is not. Do not claim a detector result, “AI-free” provenance, human review or a tool/model version that has not been confirmed.

A factual disclosure should identify: tool/product; actual model/version if documented; session date; uses performed (for example literature organization and manuscript drafting); and the human verification/revision subsequently completed. The final responsibility/review statement must be true when the authors approve the submission. No data-generation claim should be added if no data were generated by the tool.

## Public EEG ethics and study identity

The journal provides “Not applicable” as an option for work not involving humans/animals. This manuscript analyzes human EEG, so public availability alone cannot be used to infer that condition. The accurate assessment needs the source study consent/ethics record, degree of de-identification, dataset terms, and the authors' institution/jurisdictional secondary-use policy. A secondary-analysis exemption can be reported when genuinely established, with the basis described. Otherwise keep an explicit author-confirmation item rather than an invented IRB declaration.

A code repository's frozen protocol is not automatically a clinical-trial registration or prospective public preregistration. State what was fixed, on what date and relative to which data access. Do not relabel exploratory analyses, repeated development comparisons, or an interrupted experiment as an independent confirmatory trial.

## Free-format submission and the official templates

Initial submission is free format, provided the required sections and consistent complete references are present. Journal style is requested at revision. A clean, independently styled DOCX can therefore be used for drafting and repository sharing.

The instructions limit the journal templates to journal submission and explicitly exclude posting template-based documents on preprint servers/other websites. The locally downloaded templates should remain reference/submission assets, not be silently republished to GitHub. This restriction concerns the template, not the separately allowed practice of posting a preprint in an appropriate independent format.

- [Brain Sciences Word template](https://mdpi-res.com/files/word-templates/brainsci-template.dot), 83,044 bytes; despite the .dot filename it is a Word 2007+ OOXML package. Its current abstract instructions match the journal page.
