## Appropriate manuscript use

The generic statement that original-study investigators obtained informed consent is supported for all three cohorts by two complete primary articles and the Pan publisher excerpt. Use separate dataset citations. If listing the specific Pan protocol number, retain the secondary-provenance limitation until the primary numbered clause is directly verified, or omit that optional number from the current manuscript instead of presenting it as independently checked.

These source-study approvals are **not** an approval of the present authors' new analysis. They also do not establish that every public EEG file meets the Chinese statutory definition of irreversible anonymization. The manuscript's current factual secondary-use statement and Article32(1) citation therefore should not be upgraded to an invented CQMU waiver or blanket assertion that all public EEG is automatically exempt.

Detailed reading scopes, source URLs, downloaded-file hashes and explicit verification flags are recorded in `source_ethics_records.json` and `source_ethics_downloads.json`. Raw EEG payloads were not fetched for this ethics check.
